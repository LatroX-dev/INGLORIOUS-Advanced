# Code of conduct

Keep discussions about INGLORIOUS Advanced respectful and useful. Different experience levels and constructive criticism are welcome.

- Discuss the problem or proposal without personal attacks.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or impersonate others.
- Do not share another person's private information without permission.
- Keep posts relevant. Avoid spam and repeated unwanted promotion.

These expectations apply to this repository's issues, pull requests and other project-managed community spaces.

Report concerns to [inglorious@latrox.dev](mailto:inglorious@latrox.dev). Include enough context to explain what happened, without posting private information publicly. For software vulnerabilities, follow [Security reporting](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may request changes, remove inappropriate content or limit participation when needed. Decisions should consider the context, severity and whether the behavior is repeated.
